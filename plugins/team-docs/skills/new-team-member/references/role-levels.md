# Role Levels Reference

This document defines the role levels used in team member profiles at Udemy.

## Individual Contributor (IC) Levels

### IC1 - Junior Software Engineer
- Entry level engineer
- 0-2 years of experience
- Works on well-defined tasks with guidance
- Learning foundational skills
- **Examples**: Recent college graduates, career changers

### IC2 - Software Engineer
- Early career engineer
- 2-4 years of experience
- Works on defined features with some independence
- Growing technical depth
- **Common titles**: Software Engineer, Software Engineer II

### IC3 - Senior Software Engineer
- Mid-level engineer
- 4-7 years of experience
- Owns features end-to-end
- Mentors junior engineers
- Contributes to technical decisions
- **Common titles**: Senior Software Engineer, Senior SWE

### IC4 - Staff Software Engineer
- Senior technical contributor
- 7-12 years of experience
- Leads technical initiatives across team
- Defines architecture and technical direction
- Mentors engineers at all levels
- **Common titles**: Staff Software Engineer, Staff Engineer

### IC5 - Senior Staff Software Engineer
- Senior technical leader
- 12+ years of experience
- Leads organization-wide technical initiatives
- Sets technical strategy
- Industry-recognized expertise
- **Common titles**: Senior Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer

### IC6 - Principal / Distinguished Engineer
- Executive-level technical leader
- 15+ years of experience
- Company-wide technical influence
- Defines engineering standards and practices
- **Rare level**: Very few at this level

## Management (M) Levels

### M2 - Engineering Manager
- Manages a team of 4-8 engineers
- 0-2 years of management experience (but typically 5+ years as IC)
- Responsible for team delivery and people development
- Works with product on roadmap planning
- **Common titles**: Engineering Manager, EM

### M3 - Senior Engineering Manager
- Manages a team of 6-10 engineers or multiple teams
- 2-5 years of management experience
- Responsible for pod/squad delivery
- Strategic planning and cross-team coordination
- **Common titles**: Senior Engineering Manager, Senior EM

### M4 - Director of Engineering
- Manages 2-4 teams (15-40 engineers)
- 5-10 years of management experience
- Organizational planning and strategy
- Budget and headcount planning
- **Common titles**: Director, Director of Engineering

### M5 - Senior Director / VP of Engineering
- Manages multiple directors (50-100+ engineers)
- 10+ years of management experience
- Executive-level decision making
- Defines org structure and strategy
- **Common titles**: Senior Director, VP of Engineering

## Specialty Roles

### TPM - Technical Program Manager
- Coordinates cross-team initiatives
- Manages technical programs and timelines
- **No IC/M level**: Uses standard levels (TPM1, TPM2, etc.)

### Designer
- Product designers, UX researchers
- **Levels**: Designer 1, Designer 2, Senior Designer, Staff Designer

### Product Manager
- Product strategy and roadmap
- **Levels**: PM1, PM2, Senior PM, Principal PM

## How to Use in Profiles

### Format in README
```markdown
- **Role:** {Title}
- **Level:** {Level Code}
```

### Examples
```markdown
- **Role:** Software Engineer
- **Level:** IC2

- **Role:** Staff Software Engineer
- **Level:** IC4

- **Role:** Engineering Manager
- **Level:** M2
```

### Alternative: Combined
```markdown
- **Role:** IC4 Staff Software Engineer
- **Role:** M3 Senior Engineering Manager
```

## Location Information

### Common Locations in Team
- **Austin, TX** - Udemy US HQ
- **San Francisco, CA** - Udemy original location
- **Istanbul, Turkey** - International office
- **Mexico City, MX** / **CDMX** - Latin America team
- **Remote, CA** - Remote but based in California
- **Remote, OR** - Remote but based in Oregon
- **Remote** - Fully remote, location varies

### Format
Use consistent format:
- City, State (for US): "Austin, TX"
- City, Country (for international): "Istanbul, Turkey"
- Remote with state: "Remote, CA"
- Fully remote: "Remote"

```markdown

```

Examples:
- **Hire Date:** 2021-06-07
- **Hire Date:** 2025-12-15
- **Hire Date:** TBD (if not yet hired)

## Email Format

### Standard Pattern
```
firstname.lastname@udemy.com
```

### Variations
- Shortened first name: `alex.hwang@udemy.com` (not alexander)
- Middle initial: `john.a.smith@udemy.com`
- Hyphenated last name: `maria.garcia-lopez@udemy.com`
- Full middle name: `james.michael.davis@udemy.com`

Always confirm actual email rather than assuming the pattern.

## GitHub Handle Conventions

- No @ symbol in README (links will add it)
- Case-sensitive (preserve as registered on GitHub)
- Common patterns:
  - `firstnamelastname` (lowercase)
  - `firstname-lastname`
  - `firstinitiallastname`
  - May include numbers or special characters

Examples:
- bodureyupcan
- jasonmdiaz
- phamcharles
- Alex-Hwang

## Validation Checklist

When creating a new profile, verify:
- [ ] Name is spelled correctly
- [ ] GitHub handle is valid (check github.com/{handle})
- [ ] Role level matches company standards (IC1-IC6, M2-M5)
- [ ] Location format is consistent with existing profiles
- [ ] Email follows pattern and is verified
- [ ] Team/pod assignment is accurate
- [ ] Hire date is in YYYY-MM-DD format
